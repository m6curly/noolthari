Shipping Policy

Orders are prepared for dispatch only after successful payment confirmation.

Order processing

After payment is successfully verified, your order enters the fulfilment process. Processing and dispatch times may vary depending on product availability, order volume and operational requirements.

Delivery

Delivery timelines depend on the destination, courier service and other factors that may affect transportation. Delivery estimates are therefore indicative and may change.

Tracking

When tracking information is available, it will be shown in the shipment details of your order.

Delivery information

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delivery delays or unsuccessful delivery attempts.

Payment and dispatch

NOOLTHARI™ does not dispatch orders on the basis of a payment attempt alone. An order proceeds only after the payment has been successfully verified by the payment system.

Questions about your shipment

For questions about an order or shipment, please contact NOOLTHARI™ through our Contact page and keep your order number available.
